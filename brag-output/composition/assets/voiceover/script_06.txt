While all that happens, the gateway keeps a flight recorder. A span is one timed step: a name, a stage, a duration, a status, and attributes. A trace is the full journey of one request — every span sharing its id. The gateway exposes a span sink — a pluggable hook for wherever spans should go — and the demo plugs in a collector that writes them to a file. Four queries become four traces, and forty four spans. That file is the evidence. Now — who reads it?
