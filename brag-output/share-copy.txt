Eight AI systems that never talked to each other are just a collection. PlatformDemo is the proof they compose — one offline command walks a real technician question through the gateway (rate-limited, cached, metered down to the token), the flight recorder, forensics that catch a deliberately planted retrieval failure, and a release gate that passes the healthy model with exit 0 and fails the degraded one with exit 1. This whiteboard lecture follows that one request end to end: 11 tests, 44 spans, 6,578 tokens billed — an architecture claim you can rerun in 200 milliseconds.
